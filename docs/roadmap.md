# Roadmap

Order of work after the MVP. Nothing here is started.

## Before real data is trusted

1. Read the four chains' terms of use (`limits.md`).
2. First live run of Dirk, Albert Heijn, Jumbo and Vomar, checked once against the pages before each adapter is switched on.
3. Vomar (issue #6): the adapter reads the flyer PDF; watch the first weeks for a change of layout.
4. Hide a chain's demo rows once its real adapter is on.

## Next

- A regular-price source (Checkjebon open data) so comparison covers products that are not on offer.
- Barcodes and brands from Open Food Facts, to make links between chains reliable.
- Jumbo: the offers that load in the browser, if that can be done within the rules.
- Offline shopping list.
- Remember the plan settings on the device.
- Remove the lists of devices that have not returned for a year.

## Parked (see `decisions.md` §1)

Recipes and weekly budget, receipt scanning, price alerts and a savings dashboard, store map with distance and opening hours (OpenStreetMap), accounts and sync across devices, further chains, Polish, Turkish and Arabic translations.

## Only with a deployment

Hosting with an always-on worker, backups, monitoring, a privacy policy and cookie notice. A public launch needs legal advice and an agreement or official feed from at least one chain first.
